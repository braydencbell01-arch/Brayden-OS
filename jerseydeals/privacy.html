Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 23, 2026

Jersey Deals (“we”, “us”) operates the Jersey Deals storefront and related tools used to manage our own apparel listings. This policy explains what information we handle.

Who this covers

This site is our brand storefront. If we connect eBay APIs, we use them to access our own seller account data (such as our listings and inventory), not to collect personal data about other eBay buyers or sellers for resale or profiling.

Information we may process

  • Email addresses you submit for Jersey Deals restock alerts, checkout, or offers. That list is for Jersey Deals only (not BrayStats or other products). Signups are sent to our Jersey Deals inbox (via FormSubmit or a configured form endpoint) and may also be stored in your browser for checkout convenience.
  • Information you send us directly (for example order details through our store).
  • Technical data needed to run the website (basic server/browser logs such as IP address, browser type, and pages viewed).
  • Seller-account data from eBay that we are authorized to access for operating Jersey Deals (listings, inventory, order details for our shop).

What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not persist personal data of unrelated eBay users beyond what is required to run our own store operations.
  • We do not use eBay data for advertising networks or data brokerage.

Cookies and analytics

The marketing site may use essential cookies needed for the page to work. If we add analytics later, we will update this policy.

Third-party services

Payments and checkout may be handled by providers such as Shopify, Square, or eBay. Their privacy policies apply to data they collect during checkout.

Data retention

We keep business records only as long as needed for orders, support, legal requirements, or operating the store. We delete or anonymize data when it is no longer needed.

Your choices

Contact us to ask questions about this policy or to request access/deletion of personal information we hold about you, subject to legal exceptions.
